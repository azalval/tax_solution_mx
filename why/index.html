Three Core Customer Problems

What PFAE owners experience every month

Every one of these problems repeats on a 30-day cycle — and the current solution is the same in all three cases: call your contador.

01
No visibility into tax liability
Business owners have zero real-time view of what they will owe the SAT. The number arrives as a surprise — typically 2–3 days before the filing deadline — leaving no time to plan cash flow or dispute errors.
"I find out what I owe the SAT the same day my accountant sends the transfer."
02
Paying an accountant for a black box
Most PFAE filers pay $700–1,500 MXN/month to an accountant who handles filing but delivers no explanation. The business owner is left with a tax bill, no breakdown, and no way to verify the number is correct.
"I trust my contador but I have no idea if I'm being overcharged on taxes."
03
Manual data collection and handoff
Every month, the business owner must gather invoices, bank statements, and expense receipts — then send them by email or WhatsApp to the accountant. The accountant re-keys everything manually. No automation, no audit trail.
"I spend 2 weekends every month collecting information for my contador."

The Solution

Fiscal-iA replaces the accountant workflow end-to-end

Seven integrated capabilities that cover every step of the monthly compliance journey — from CFDI ingestion to SAT filing to bank payment.

📊 Real-time tax visibility
MTD income, deductible expenses, and estimated ISR and IVA updated live from CFDIs and bank data. No more end-of-month surprises — the number is always visible.
🤖 AI-powered CFDI validation
Flags wrong 'uso del CFDI' codes, unmatched cancelations, and non-deductible expenses. Validates every CFDI against SAT business rules before it enters the tax calculation.
📋 Pre-filled SAT declaration
All ISR and IVA form fields auto-populated from CFDIs, payroll, and adjustments. Trend comparisons vs. prior 3 months highlight anomalies before submission.
🔐 One-click filing with e.firma
Fiscal-iA presents both ISR and IVA directly to the SAT portal using the user's stored e.firma — no portal sessions, no captchas, no manual form entry.
💳 Managed payment (5% fee)
After filing, Fiscal-iA executes the bank transfer to the SAT on the user's behalf. Total taxes + 5% commission charged to their linked account.
📉 Cancellation impact engine
Tracks pending CFDI cancelations and calculates in real-time how each one affects monthly tax liability — so the user can act before the deadline, not after.
🧾 Regime-aware deductibility engine
Automatically classifies each expense as deductible or non-deductible based on the user's regime (PFAE or RESICO) and the latest SAT policy. Highlights eligible deducciones autorizadas, flags expenses with the wrong CFDI 'uso' code for their regime, and surfaces missed deductions — updating in real-time as SAT rules change.

Challenges & Risks

Three structural challenges

These are not hypothetical risks — they are live constraints that require deliberate product and engineering strategies, not workarounds.

Behavioral
🤝 Strong attachment to accountants
PFAE business owners have relied on their contador for years — often with a personal, trust-based relationship. Moving to a DIY model requires overcoming inertia, fear of SAT errors, and deeply ingrained habits. The risk is that users adopt the dashboard but still delegate filing to their accountant rather than replacing them.
Mitigation: Position Fiscal-iA as a complement first ("audit your contador"), not a replacement. Build trust through accuracy and transparency before pushing full DIY. Introduce the Fiscal Expert Network as a safety net for complex cases.
Technical
🔌 No open SAT filing API
The SAT does not offer a public API for tax submission. The only available mechanism is filling and submitting forms through their web portal after manual e.firma authentication. This means Fiscal-iA must automate browser-based interactions (RPA/scraping) — a fragile approach that can break with any SAT UI change, and raises compliance questions around credential handling.
Mitigation: Build a resilient e.firma session layer with change detection and fallback prompts. Engage SAT's digital transformation teams to advocate for open APIs. Design the UX so users feel in control of the submission step.
Infrastructure
⏳ Asynchronous CFDI retrieval from SAT
El Web Service del SAT no entrega las facturas de forma inmediata. Opera mediante un sistema de solicitudes asíncronas con tres métodos SOAP: SolicitaDescarga, VerificaSolicitudDescarga y DescargaMasiva. Una sola solicitud puede tardar varias horas en completarse y tiene límites de registros por solicitud — lo que complica la sincronización en tiempo real que el dashboard de Fiscal-iA requiere.
Mitigation: Implement a background polling engine with intelligent retry logic and local CFDI caching. Show users a "last synced" timestamp and allow manual refresh. Pre-fetch CFDIs nightly so data is ready before month-end crunch.
